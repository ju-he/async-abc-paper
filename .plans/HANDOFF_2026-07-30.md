   5 reps had *finished* inside the 180 s wall; only the trailing
   `--finalize-only` timed out, because `rebuild_scaling_outputs` loaded every
   `raw_results_w*_k*.csv` shard (~150M records / >10 GB) and all six jobs raced
   to do it. `--finalize-skip-records` restored the aggregates in full at **zero
   node-hours**, instead of the full grid re-run the previous handoff proposed.

2. **Limitation (vii) is now fully closed** — it had three open limits at the
   start of the session. twinB (barrier every 112 ≈ the baseline's population of
   100) showed the barrier's straggler cost is granularity-independent
   (5×/10×/20× agree to 3 s.f. with the fine twin); the heterogeneity twin closed
   the second; the CPM-scale twin closed the third. The twin now isolates
   synchronization on **all three** benchmarks where systems gains are reported:
   21–402× (straggler), 1.1–25× (heterogeneity), 1.9–4.4× (Cellular Potts). What
   remains is scoped as a limitation of the twin as an *instrument* (it deadlocks
   at ≥192 ranks), not as an attribution gap.

   The CPM result's second finding is the more interesting one: the barrier makes
   throughput **unpredictable**, not just lower — 27–75% CV across seeds against
   the asynchronous arm's 0.6–1.3%, on identical proposals and identical seeds,
   because a generation inherits the cost of its most expensive parameter draw.

   archive size does *not* trade calibration against throughput (k=100 dominates
   every larger archive on both axes at once), and the cross-algorithm

**Two rules out of this:** before "fixing" a stale artifact, check that both
sides use the same checkpoint strategy *and* the same aggregation; and before
attributing a difference to a strategy, verify it actually moves the number.

## Hard-won gotchas — do not rediscover

- **On a TIMEOUT/FAILED job, check whether the simulation work finished** before
  concluding data loss. `scaling_cpm_single.sh` marks the whole job FAILED if
  *any* per-combo srun fails, and the k-frontier's TIMEOUTs were all in the
  finalize.
- **The barrierized twin intermittently deadlocks at ≥192 ranks** (2 of 6 CPM
  replicates; one wedged in teardown with "191 pending intra-island send
  requests", one after 2 evaluations). It is intermittent — the w384 replicate
  that hung completed on retry. Cap the step (`--step-timeout-min`) so a hang
  costs 60 min on 8 nodes, not 120.
- **Only the barrier arm needs sim-limiting.** A collective barrier needs
  identical per-rank call counts; wall-clock stops ranks independently and
  deadlocks. Async runs wall-limited. The two are compared as **rates**
  (sims / `active_wall_time_s`), never as a matched budget.
- **The twin never has to fit the async arm's wall budget** — this is what made
  the previous handoff think σ=2 heterogeneity was infeasible. It is a ~1200 s
  per-replicate run.
- **Size per-cell budgets from measured rates.** The heterogeneity rate spans
  117× from σ=0 to σ=2; one monolithic `max_simulations` cannot serve both.
- **Never give both arms the same budget** (per-worker budgets make both arms
  straggler-gated and erase the effect).
- **Verify which run a scratch path belongs to.** The paper's LV/CPM scaling
  data is `rerun_20260707`; several figure scripts still point at pre-rerun runs.
- `sbc_`-prefixed experiments resolve by convention; every other new experiment
  needs entries in **all three** registries (`run_all_paper_experiments`,
  `shard_finalizers`, `sharding`) — plus watch the hidden allowlists
  (`schema.VALID_BENCHMARK_NAMES`, `submit_replicate_shards`'s own check).
- Summary CSVs name the tagged run **`method`** (straggler/hetero) or
  **`method_variant`** (scaling) — a repair pass keyed on one silently skips
  the other. That naming also tells you which checkpoint strategy produced the
  row: `method` → `quantile`/8 (`runtime_summary`), `method_variant` → `all`
  (`scaling_runner`).
- **NaN in a scaling per-combination shard is a deferred marker, not a missing
  value.** The runners defer the quality metric to `_backfill_quality_metrics`
  at finalize; anything that fills those NaNs makes the next finalize skip its
  own backfill.
- `submit_scaling_cpm.py` takes `output_dir` **positionally**; its walltime
  estimator returns 0 s for sim-limited runs → pass `--min-time`.
- rsync deploys must use **full destination paths**.
